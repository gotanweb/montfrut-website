Privacy

Who operates this site

This website is operated by Montfrut, the registered trade name (DBA) of a sole proprietorship operating in New Jersey, United States. For privacy inquiries, contact montfrut@gmail.com.

Statistics and their purpose

Montfrut uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) on Links and the Catalog to understand visits and which sections interest visitors, and to improve the site. On Links, we record page visits and activations of its nine links, with a button identifier, group and page language. A WhatsApp click does not mean that a message was sent or a purchase was made.

The Catalog records only page views and language, including changes between its ES/EN routes. Customer names, companies, event dates, comments, selection details and inquiry text are not sent. The form is not saved to change language. Commercial consent before WhatsApp or copying an inquiry is separate from this statistics preference.

GA4 may process technical browser and device information and cookie identifiers. When connecting to Google, your browser transmits technical information, including your IP address. Our custom events do not include names, email addresses, telephone numbers, WhatsApp messages or URL parameters. Advertising categories remain denied.

In Google Analytics, retention for event data and user data associated with identifiers is set to 14 months, without resetting upon new activity.

Cookies and storage

When statistics are enabled, GA4 may use cookies to distinguish visits. Your explicit choice is saved in your browser's local storage under montfrut.analyticsConsent; the language preference uses montfrut.language. The statistics preference contains no personal data and can be shared by modules on the same origin when they adopt this management system. If your browser blocks storage, your choice can only be maintained on the current page.

Statistics require your explicit acceptance before Google Analytics loads, regardless of the country you visit from. If there is no saved choice, we show the notice. You can accept, reject or change your choice at any time on this page. If you reject, no new measurements are made through this integration.

Your preferences

You can accept or reject statistics at any time. This page does not load GA4. Your choice applies to Links and the Catalog in this browser and origin; open tabs update when storage is available.

Rejecting stops new measurements from this integration. It does not delete data Google Analytics has already received.
