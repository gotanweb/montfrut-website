Statistics and their purpose

Montfrut uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) on Links to understand visits and which sections interest visitors, and to improve the site. We record page visits and activations of its nine links, with a button identifier, group and page language. A WhatsApp click does not mean that a message was sent or a purchase was made.

GA4 may process technical browser and device information and cookie identifiers. When connecting to Google, your browser transmits technical information, including your IP address. Our custom events do not include names, email addresses, telephone numbers, WhatsApp messages or URL parameters. Advertising categories remain denied.

Cookies, storage and region

When statistics are enabled, GA4 may use cookies to distinguish visits. Your explicit choice is saved in your browser's local storage under montfrut.analyticsConsent; the language preference uses montfrut.language. The statistics preference contains no personal data and can be shared by modules on the same origin when they adopt this management system. If your browser blocks storage, your choice can only be maintained on the current page.

The server uses GeoIP to determine the country and returns only a country code or an unknown result. In the EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland, other countries or if detection fails, permission is requested before loading GA4. In the United States, under the configured regional rule, statistics may be enabled without a previous choice; this is not saved as explicit acceptance. A previous rejection is respected, and permission is requested if your browser communicates Global Privacy Control. The applicability of the US rule is awaiting approval.

Your preferences

You can accept or reject statistics at any time. This page does not load GA4. Your choice applies to Links in this browser and origin; open tabs update when storage is available.

Rejecting stops new measurements from this integration. It does not delete data Google Analytics has already received.
