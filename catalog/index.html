Allergy Information

At Montfrut, we take food safety seriously. Please be aware that our products are handcrafted in a facility that processes common allergens.

  • Dairy: Many of our chocolates contain cream and butter.
  • Nuts: We use hazelnuts, pistachios, and almonds in various recipes.
  • Soy: Some ingredients may contain soy lecithin as an emulsifier.
  • Gluten: While many products are gluten-free, cross-contamination is possible.

For specific ingredient questions, please check the details of each individual product or contact our team.
